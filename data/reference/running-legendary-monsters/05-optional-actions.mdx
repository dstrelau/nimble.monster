### Optional Actions

Each legendary monster can also have the default actions to cause fear or move players around instead of their listed attacks. A great way to add drama or tune down an encounter that is too challenging. For example:

* **Wind Up/Breathe In.** Regain the use of a single use ability.
* **Terrible Roar/Creepy Monologue/Taunt.** Creatures (or a creature) who hear this make a WIL save or are {{term:Frightened}} for 1 turn.
* **Toss Around/Telekinetic Shove.** STR save or moved, {{term:Prone}}, etc.
* **Size Up/Spot Weakness/Pin Down.** DEX save, the next attack you make is with {{term:advantage}} and cannot be Interposed.
