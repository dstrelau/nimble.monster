### Turns, Rounds & Encounters

A **Turn** is when 1 individual hero or monster group acts. A typical turn takes roughly 6 seconds of “in-world“ time. A **Round** is when all players and monsters have taken a turn acting. An **Encounter** is all of the rounds in a particular combat.

**1/Turn Abilities.**
If you perform one on your turn, and can find a way to perform it on another creature’s turn, you can do it again (e.g., Sneak Attack combined with Opportunity Attack or Coordinated Strike!).

**1/Round Abilities.**
These reset whenever your own turn begins (e.g., when you Defend, you cannot do so again until your turn comes back around).
