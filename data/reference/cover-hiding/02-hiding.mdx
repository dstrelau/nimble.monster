### Hiding

To hide in combat, you must have Cover from the creatures you are attempting to hide from and use an action to make a **DC 15 Stealth** check (if you have Full Cover, you succeed automatically). The first attack you make while hidden is made with {{term:advantage}}, then you are no longer hidden. If this attack kills the enemy and no other enemy can see you, you may remain hidden instead.
