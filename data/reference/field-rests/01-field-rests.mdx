# Field Rests

While out adventuring, heroes can take Field Rests to regain HP. 

**Catch Breath.**
Requires at least 10 minutes to tend to your injuries. Expend any number of {{term:Hit Dice}} one at a time (roll them and add your STR to each), and regain that many HP.

**Make Camp.**
If you rest for at least 8 hours with food and sleep, take the maximum value for each Hit Die you expend instead of rolling. Add your STR to each Hit Die as usual.

> What if I Have Negative STR?
> Your frail body doesn’t recover as quickly as others. Subtract your STR from each HD you expend.
