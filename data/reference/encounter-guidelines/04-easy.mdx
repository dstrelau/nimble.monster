### Easy

If the monster’s total levels are less than half of the heroes’, this is an easy encounter. Heroes will lose minimal HP and resources, making these encounters great for testing new abilities or gauging progress—or whittling down resources. They help players feel powerful, especially after {{term:leveling up}}. Use 1–2 easy encounters in a typical session.
