### Lore (INT)

Your understanding of the history of civilization, kingdoms, and religions. Use Lore to recall historical events and grasp the significance of cultural practices. It extends to knowledge of the
abilities and behavior of Celestials, Dragons, Fey, Fiends, Giants, Humanoids, and Undead.
