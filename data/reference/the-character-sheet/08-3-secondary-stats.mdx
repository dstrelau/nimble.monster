### 3. Secondary Stats

Mark your max HP and {{term:Hit Dice}} size (see your class), Hit Die amount (default: equal to your level), Initiative (default: DEX), Size, {{term:Speed}} (default: 6), max {{term:Wounds}} (default: 6), and inventory slots (10+STR).
