### Improvised Weapons

Using an improvised weapon (e.g. a chair, a frying pan, a big rock), default to {{dice:1d4+STR}} or {{dice:1d6+STR}}. Your GM may allow you to use bigger dice or other properties if it makes sense. An improvised weapon may be likely to break if it lands a crit, though!
