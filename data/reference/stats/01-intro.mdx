title: "Stats"
---
Heroes have 4 stats that impact their effectiveness in various tasks: 2 Key Stats crucial to their class and 2 Secondary Stats. The maximum a hero’s stat can typically go is +5.

**KEY.**
When an ability or spell references “KEY,” use one of your Key Stats. If a stat is listed before a die roll, roll a number of dice equal to the stat. For example, “WIL d8” means that if your WIL is 2, you roll 2d8 (two eight-sided dice).
