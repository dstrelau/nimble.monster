Defense represents your hero’s ability to dodge or block damage when you use the {{term:Defend}} reaction. While unarmored or wearing regular clothes, your Defense is equal to your DEX. Some items may grant additional Defense while equipped.

> Defend Yourself!
> If you’re standing right next to a baddie with an axe and you’re not doing anything to defend yourself, chances are, you’re going to get axed!
