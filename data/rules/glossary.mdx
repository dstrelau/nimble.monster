- **{{term:Advantage}} X.** Roll X additional dice and drop the X lowest dice (removing dice from left to right in the case of a tie).
- **Ally.** A friendly creature, not yourself.
- **AoE.** (Area of Effect). Special attacks that target an area rather than individual creatures (see “Reach, Range, & AoEs” on pg. 10).
- **Assess.** An action to strategically analyze the situation in combat (see “Assess” on pg. 13).
- **Attack.** Any offensive action. Swinging with a weapon, casting a harmful spell, {{term:Grappling}}, etc.
- **Basic Attack.** A cantrip or weapon attack.
- **{{term:Blind}}.** Can’t see. Attacks against you have advantage, and your attacks have disadvantage.
- **Blindsight X.** You can sense creatures and obstacles normally within X spaces, ignoring Blind, darkness, and invisibility.
- **{{term:Bloodied}}.** At half HP or less.
- **Burrowing.** When a creature can move normally underground.
- **Cantrip.** A basic spell. Costs 0 mana to cast, and their power increases as you level up.
- **{{term:Charged}}.** Some spells/abilities have additional effects when Charged (e.g., Overload, Electrickery). Become Charged whenever you take lightning damage. Lasts 1 min or until combat ends.
- **{{term:Charmed}}.** Sees the charmer as an ally. Charmer has advantage on social interactions with you.
- **Climbing.** A creature with a climbing Speed can move across vertical surfaces as flat ground, otherwise climbing is treated as Difficult Terrain.
- **{{term:Concentration}}.** Can only Concentrate on 1 thing at a time. DC 10 STR save when crit or it breaks (see “Concentration” on pg. 8).
- **Cone X.** Starts at the character and extends X spaces outward, becoming up to X spaces wide at the farthest edge.
- **Cover.** If mostly obscured, incoming attacks have disadvantage. If completely obscured (Full Cover), cannot typically be targeted.
- **Crit.** Whenever a creature rolls the max on a Primary Die, that die is rolled again and added to the total.
- **d44, d66, d88.** Roll 2 of the same dice, the leftmost die is the tens place, and the second is the ones. These attacks do not miss or crit.
- **Damage Types.** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Radiant, Necrotic, and Psychic. Heroes typically do not care what type of damage they take, but some monsters or environmental challenges do.
- **Darkvision X.** Can see normally in the dark, up to X spaces.
- **{{term:Dazed}}.** Heroes: lose 1 action; monsters: can perform one less action on their next turn.
- **Decrement/Increment.** Use a die one size smaller or larger (d4 » d6 » d8 » d10 » d12 » d20).
- **Difficult Terrain.** Speed halved while here.
- **{{term:Disadvantage}} X.** Roll X additional dice and drop the X highest dice (removing dice from left to right in the case of a tie).
- **Distracted.** A target is distracted if it is adjacent to or {{term:Taunted}} by an ally, or if it cannot see you.
- **{{term:Dying}}.** When a hero is at 0 HP (see “Hit Points & Dying” on pg. 9).
- **Encounter.** An encounter begins when Initiative is rolled and ends when hostilities do.
- **Falling.** Suffer 1d6 damage/2 spaces fallen.
- **{{term:Field Rest}}/Safe Rest.** Spend time to recover some of your resources (see “Resting & Downtime” on pg. 16).
- **Free.** Does not cost an action or any other resource (e.g., mana) unless otherwise stated.
- **{{term:Frightened}}.** Disadvantage on rolls when source of fear is near; Speed halved when moving closer to it.
- **{{term:Grappled}}.** Cannot move. Attacks against you have advantage (see “Grappling” on pg. 10).
- **{{term:Hampered}}.** Any creature with actions or movement reduced (e.g., Dazed, Grappled/Restrained, Prone, Slowed, in Difficult Terrain).
- **{{term:Incapacitated}}.** Can’t do anything. Attacks against you have advantage, and melee attacks that hit, crit.
- **{{term:Invisible}}.** Cannot be seen. Your attacks have advantage, and attacks against you have disadvantage.
- **KEY.** The two most important stats for a class. Replace with either of your KEY attributes.
- **Knockback/Push X.** Forcibly move a creature X spaces, ignoring Difficult Terrain. Other creatures or the environment may halt movement prematurely and deal damage (see “Falling & Forced Movement” on pg. 10).
- **Line X.** A straight line extending up to X spaces in one direction.
- **LVL.** Level. When part of an attack, replace it with the character’s level.
- **Mana.** Used to fuel spellcasting (see “Magic” on pg. 44). See the individual Hero classes for more info on how they gain mana.
- **Minion.** Simple creatures: any damage kills them; each attacks with a single damage die, cannot crit, and misses on a roll of 1; when multiple minions attack a single target, their damage is combined and counts as a single attack.
- **Move X.** Move up to that many spaces.
- **Paralyzed, Stunned, Unconscious.** Incapacitated. Can’t do anything. Attacks against you have advantage, and melee attacks that hit, crit.
- **{{term:Petrified}}.** Incapacitated with all the benefits and drawbacks of being a rock! Immune to most damage except from large explosions, picks, or similar tools.
- **{{term:Poisoned}}.** Disadvantage on rolls (typically, healing ends).
- **Primary Die.** The leftmost die in an attack, this die determines if an attack misses or crits.
- **{{term:Prone}}.** Movement costs twice as much, and disadvantage on attacks. Melee attacks against you have advantage; Ranged have disadvantage. Spend 3 spaces of your Speed to stand up.
- **Range X.** Ranged attacks are made with disadvantage if ANY enemy is adjacent. Range can be extended (see “Reach, Range, & AoEs” on pg. 10).
- **Reach X.** The area within X spaces around a character; forming your choice of a cube or sphere.
- **Resistance.** Take –10 damage vs a specific damage type.
- **{{term:Restrained}}.** (Same as Grappled; objects Restrain, creatures Grapple) Cannot move. Attacks against you have advantage.
- **{{term:Riding}}.** You move with the creature you are riding. Any attacks that miss you, strike them (see “Grappling” on pg. 10).
- **Round.** The time in combat when each Hero and enemy has a chance to act (see “Turns, Rounds, & Encounters” on pg. 15).
- **Rushed Attacks.** Attacking more than once on a turn imposes a cumulative disadvantage penalty (e.g., attacking with a short sword for the third time on a turn, you would roll 3d6 and take the lowest result).
- **{{term:Silenced}}.** Cannot cast spells or use other abilities that require speaking (e.g., Commander’s Orders).
- **Skill Points.** Use them to increase your skills. Start with 4 at level 1 and gain 1 each level (see “Leveling Up” on pg. 21).
- **{{term:Slowed}}.** Speed halved during your next turn.
- **{{term:Smoldering}}.** This condition does nothing on its own, though some spells and abilities have additional effects against Smoldering creatures, ending the condition (e.g., Ignite, Pyroclasm).
- **Spellcasting Focus.** Can be used instead of an empty hand to cast spells.
- **Stats.** STR, DEX, INT, WIL: the core attributes of a character.
- **Surprise.** When combat starts before all sides are aware (see “Starting Combat” on pg. 15).
- **Target.** A selected creature or object. It must be within Range/Reach and able to be sensed.
- **{{term:Taunted}}.** Disadvantage on attacks except against the most recent taunter.
- **Teleport.** Move instantaneously. Does not provoke opportunity attacks.
- **Temp HP.** HP that do not replenish (see “Hit Points & Dying” on pg. 9).
- **Turn.** The primary time allotted for an individual hero or monster group to act.
- **Unheld.** Not touched, worn, or held by anyone.
- **Upcast.** Spend extra mana on a spell to gain an extra effect for each additional mana spent.
- **Vicious.** Roll 1 additional die whenever you roll crit damage.
- **Vulnerable.** When a creature is vulnerable to a damage type, it takes +10 damage.
- **{{term:Wound}}.** A measure of how close a hero is to death. Typically 6 and you’re dead (see “Hit Points & Dying” on pg. 9).
- **{{term:Wounded}}.** Has any Wounds.
