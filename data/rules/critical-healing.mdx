Treat healing just like an attack roll. Rolling the max is a crit (roll again and add it to the result), rolling 1 is a failure to heal (note: this variant is FUN for the right group that enjoys big, dramatic, swingy moments). Consider incrementing the die size by one step to compensate if you use this variant (d4 » d6 » d8 » d10 » d12).
