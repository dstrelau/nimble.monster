Whenever a party or hero does something memorable (role-plays a great moment, makes everyone laugh, misses an attack multiple times in a row, or otherwise engages in positive behavior), the GM can grant Inspiration: the ability to reroll any single die. Inspiration expires after a Safe Rest.
