Just like weapons, spells with “Range” can target creatures from a distance, but are cast with {{term:disadvantage}} if any enemy is adjacent to you. Add 1 die of {{term:disadvantage}} to gain +2 Range (max +6). Spells with “Reach” can’t do this, but they are cast as normal even when in melee.
