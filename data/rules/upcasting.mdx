---
title: "Upcasting"
keywords: ["tier", "mana", "additional", "spells"]
sources:
  - book: "Core Rules v2"
    pages: [45]
---

You can spend additional mana on Tiered spells to make them stronger for each additional mana spent (up to the Tier that you have unlocked). For example, a character that has Tier 3 spells unlocked can upcast a Tier 1 or 2 spell at Tier 3 by spending 3 mana on them.
