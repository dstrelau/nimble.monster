---
title: "Interpose"
category: combat
sources:
  - book: "Core Rules"
    pages: [14, 55]
---

If a creature within Reach 2 would be hit with an attack, you can enter their space and become the new target of the attack. They move to an adjacent space of their choice.

If the ally can be pushed out of the affected area, an AoE attack can be Interposed. Interpose also works with conditional attacks when it makes sense that you could push the ally out of the way (for example, if the ally is not Grappled); the Interposing hero suffers the damage and all other effects as normal.

> Interpose AND Defend?
> Yes! As long as you have enough actions to spend, you can Interpose and Defend at the same time. However, you won’t be able to do either reaction again until your next turn ends since each is limited to 1/round.
