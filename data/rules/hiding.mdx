---
title: "Hiding"
category: core-rules
sources:
  - book: "Core Rules"
    pages: [8]
---

To hide in combat, you must have Cover from the creatures you are attempting to hide from and use an action to make a **DC 10 Stealth** check (if you have Full Cover, you succeed automatically). The first attack you make while hidden is made with {{term:advantage}}, then you are no longer hidden. Most creatures will not prioritize hunting for hidden heroes while other dangers remain.
