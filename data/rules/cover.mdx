A creature mostly obscured from line of sight (standing behind a tree, a larger ally, a knocked
over table, in poor lighting, etc.) has **Cover** and imposes {{term:disadvantage}} on attacks against them. A creature completely obscured from view has **Full Cover** and cannot typically be targeted by an attack.
