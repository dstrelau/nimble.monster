For activities that cost more than 1 action, your actions can be spent over multiple turns in combat as long as {{term:Concentration}} is maintained and you do not perform other actions or reactions in the meantime (unless they are free).

> Example
> Book, the Mage, wants to cast Glacier Strike (3 actions), but only has 1 action left. He’ll spend 1 action on this turn to start casting the spell, and on his next turn spend 2 more to finish casting it. He’s taking a risk, though: he can’t Defend himself and may need to rely on his party to protect him.
