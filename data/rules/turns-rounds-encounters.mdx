A **Turn** is when a hero or group of monsters acts. A **Round** is when all creatures have taken a turn. Creatures act simultaneously “in-world,” so Rounds and Turns both take roughly 6 seconds. An **Encounter** is all of the rounds in a particular combat.

**1/Turn Abilities.**
If you perform one on your turn, and can find a way to perform it on another creature’s turn, you can do it again (e.g., Sneak Attack combined with Opportunity Attack or Coordinated Strike!).

**1/Round Abilities.**
These reset whenever your own turn ends (e.g., when you Defend, you cannot do so again until your next turn ends).

Spells and abilities such as Heart’s Fire and Electrocharge can bring you above your normal action cap.
