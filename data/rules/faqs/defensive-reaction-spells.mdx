---
question: "How do defensive and reaction spells affect their trigger?"
keywords: ["reaction timing","triggering attack","interrupt spell","prevent damage"]

Reactions resolve before their trigger. So if an attack was made but the attacker is now {{term:Poisoned}}/{{term:Frightened}}/{{term:Blind}}/etc. the attack is rolled again, but taking into account the condition (rolling with disadvantage). You can only cast 1 defensive spell each time you Defend (e.g., Frost Shield OR Alacrity, not both on the same round).
