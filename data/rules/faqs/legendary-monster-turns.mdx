What if a mob of 100 commoners attack a dragon, does it get to move at light {{term:speed}} after each one of their turns?! No. Only after hero’s turns. Commoners, minions, and other followers are not heroes. As the GM, use your best judgment and feel free to nix any rule that your table doesn’t like or that doesn’t make sense in the world.
