---
question: "How does Interpose work with conditional attacks?"
keywords: ["grapple","restrain","take the hit","attack rider","conditional effect"]
kind: official
targets: [interpose, conditions#grappled-restrained]
sources:
  - book: "Core Rules v2"

If it makes sense that the hero could push their ally out of the way of the attack (e.g., the ally isn’t Grappled), it works as normal—the Interposing hero suffers the damage and other effects as normal.
