---
question: "How does Barrier of Wind interact with Defend?"
keywords: ["wind wall","defend spell","upcast defense","ranged attacks"]

targets: [defend, upcasting, range-and-reach]
sources:

When an ally is attacked at Range, if you upcast Barrier of Wind, you can Defend to protect them provided they are within Reach.
