## Spells & Reactions

- **Defensive/Reaction Spells.** Reactions resolve before their trigger. So if an attack was made but the attacker is now Poisoned/Frightened/Blind/etc. the attack is rolled again, but taking into account the condition (rolling with disadvantage). You can only cast 1 defensive spell each time you [Defend](/rules/defend) (e.g., Frost Shield OR Alacrity, not both on the same round).
- **Barrier of Wind.** When an ally is attacked at Range, if you upcast Barrier of Wind, you can [Defend](/rules/defend) to protect them provided they are within Reach.
- **KEY.** Whenever a spell (or ability) uses KEY, it refers to the [caster’s attribute](/rules/stats), not the target’s.
- **Flat damage abilities (e.g. Condemn), or d44, d66, & d88 spells and Create an Opening?** These abilities do not have a [Primary Die](/rules/attack) so they are unaffected by Create an Opening. Instead, the next attack that has a Primary Die would be affected.

## Interposing & Attacks

- **[Interposing](/rules/interpose) & AoEs?** If one can be pushed out of the affected area, AoE attacks can be Interposed.
- **How does [Interposing](/rules/interpose) work with conditional attacks (e.g., Troll’s bite)?** If it makes sense that the hero could push their ally out of the way of the attack (e.g., the ally isn’t Grappled), it works as normal—the Interposing hero suffers the damage and other effects as normal.
- **Is {{term:Grappling}} considered an [attack](/rules/attack)?** Yes. Anything that negatively impacts your enemies is an attack and impacted by Rushed Attacks (in this case the enemy would gain stacking advantage on their save as the rushed penalty).
- **[Action Cap](/rules/heroic-actions).** Spells/Abilities like Heart’s Fire and Electrocharge can bring you above your normal action cap.
- **Warding Bond.** Yes, this counts as being attacked so you can [Defend](/rules/defend) against it as normal.
