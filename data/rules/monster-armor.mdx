---
title: "Monster Armor"
category: combat
sources:
  - book: "Core Rules"
    pages: [14]
---

Most monsters are unarmored, but some tougher foes might have:

- **Medium Armor:** –5 damage from incoming attacks.
- **Heavy Armor:** –10 damage from incoming attacks.

Crits ignore monster armor.

Some monsters may also be **Vulnerable** (+10 damage) or **Resistant** (–10 damage) to certain kinds of attacks.
