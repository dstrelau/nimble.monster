For an old school “funnel” adventure each player gets 4 commoners to play as. Whoever survives long enough gets to become a real level 1 adventurer! Treat each as a d6 minion: no HP, no Wounds, no stats, no skills, or special abilities. On a player’s turn they can move and attack with all of their commoners, and each player can Defend for 1d6 damage with a single commoner each round. If a player loses all of their commoners, you might allow them to play as a ghost which can do 1 creative thing per round (e.g., Frighten, distract, trip or possess an enemy).
