---
title: "Optional Tweaks"
category: 5e-conversion
---

* **Monster Saves.** To simplify play, roll a d20 for saves without bonuses. If a monster is notably Strong/Frail, Fast/Slow, or Smart/Dumb, give them {{term:advantage}} or {{term:disadvantage}} on their STR, DEX, or WIL/INT saves. Legendary monsters may have {{term:advantage}} on multiple or all saves.
* **Player Agency.** When a monster would take away agency in some way from heroes (incapacitate, paralyze, petrify, or stun), consider the new {{term:Dazed}} condition instead (–1 action for 1 round).
