---
title: "Multi-Target/Area of Effect (AoE)"
category: magic
---

Multi-target/AoE attacks follow special rules to streamline play: they do not miss or crit, and a single roll is applied to all targets. (Ignore (dis){{term:advantage}} from conditions on individual targets—such as {{term:Blinded}} or {{term:Grappled}}—though anything granting you (dis){{term:advantage}} still applies to your roll as normal.)
