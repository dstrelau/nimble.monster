Traverse spaces up to your {{term:speed}} this turn. This can be broken up with other actions, and you can use multiple actions to move multiple times in one turn. While in **Difficult Terrain** (e.g., water, mud, thick roots, climbing a cliffside), each space costs twice as much movement.
