Ignore the grid (occasionally, or all the time if you prefer) using 3 range bands instead (as always, the GM will judge unclear situations and which creatures are affected by abilities with an area of effect):

- **Close.** Creatures are effectively considered adjacent or close enough to be affected by melee attacks or abilities up to Reach/Range 3 without moving;
- **Midrange.** Creatures can be affected by thrown weapons and shorter range abilities (Reach/Range 4–6). Or you can move Close by moving once.
- **Far.** Creatures can be affected by longer range spells, bows, etc. You can move Close by moving twice.
