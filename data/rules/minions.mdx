Minions are weak enemies that die from any amount of damage. Their feeble attacks cannot crit, ignore {{term:advantage}}/{{term:disadvantage}}, and they attack as a group, so any number of minions can be Defended against as if they were a single attack. Dealing a large amount of damage to a minion can, at the GM’s discretion, overflow to other minions within range as well.
