---
title: "Safe Rests"
category: resting-downtime
---

Safe Rests take place in a safe location designated by your GM, typically lodging at an inn overnight—but could also be at a secret oasis, a well-stocked cabin in the woods, near a sacred shrine, or the like. Camping in the open wilderness or in a dungeon is **not** sufficient for gaining the benefits of a Safe Rest.

After a Safe Rest, heroes recover all of their HP, {{term:Hit Dice}}, mana (and other class-specific resources), and heal 1 {{term:Wound}}. Safe Rests are a great opportunity for {{term:downtime}} activities as well.

> Rest for HOW Long?!
> If your table prefers to largely skip {{term:downtime}} activities and narrate past a week of resting, that’s okay too! For a more realistic convalescence time, 1 week per {{term:wound}} recovered may make more sense.
