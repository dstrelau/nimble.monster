## Sample Rule 1
Nimble Initiative is great for teamplay and plays fast, without the need of much tracking.
Some tables might prefer a bit more back and forth between heroes & monsters and less potential for focus fire. Here are two variants one can try:


Standard 5e:
Turn order based on Initiative.
Heroes always start with 3 actions.


Zipper:
Switch between hero and monster turns
If one side heavily outnumbers the other, you can weave in multiple turns of the outnumbering side.
Heroes always start with 3 actions
High initiative (20+) upgrade 1 action to Inspired Action
Low initiative (<=9) downgrade 1 action to Bane Action

Inspired Action:
Roll with 1 advantage
Defend with +KEY armor
Move with +2 speed

Bane Action:
Roll with 1 disadvantage
Defend with -KEY armor
Move with -2 speed

## Sample Rule 2
Zone of Control is an optional rule one can use to achieve the same tactical effect in games using hexes or adjacent diagonals.

How does it work?
Every creature has a Zone of Control equal to Reach 1. Movement while in a hostile creatures Zone of Control costs 1 additional space of movement.

Or to put it more simply:
Moving while adjacent to an enemy costs 1 extra space.

This opens the space for features that increase your Zone of Control (for tanks), or features that let you ignore Zone of Control (for mobile skirmishers)
